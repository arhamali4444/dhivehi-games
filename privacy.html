Privacy Policy

Dhivehi Games · Last updated 24 September 2026

Dhivehi Games, including Digu, is made by Mohamed Arham Ali in Malé, Maldives. This page explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details (only if you register): your username and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password ourselves.
  • Game profile (guests and registered players): your display name, XP, shells and game stats such as games played and wins. Guests get an anonymous Firebase ID so progress can be saved. Your name and stats may appear on the public “Most Wins” leaderboard.
  • Online status: while a game is open on your screen, it checks in about once a minute with your anonymous account ID and the time, so the home page can show how many people are playing. It contains no name and is removed when you close the game.
  • A random player ID: created on your device so tables and reconnecting work. It is not linked to your phone number, contacts or location.
  • Reports and support messages: if you report a player or contact support, we store your message, the reason, the table code and up to ten recent chat lines from the reported player so we can review it.

Things other players can see

Your display name, avatar, score and title are visible to other players at your table. If you create a public table, your name and table code are listed in “Open tables” until the game starts.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites. We don’t sell your information.

Chat and online games

Games with friends and Quick Match connect players through public relay servers. Game moves and chat are encrypted between players’ devices, and chat is not saved on our servers. Chat is filtered for offensive words, links and phone numbers. You can mute, block or report any player by tapping their avatar during a game.

Services we use

To run the game, your device connects to: Google Firebase (accounts and profiles), Google Fonts (text styles), and public relay servers from EMQX and HiveMQ (online tables). Like any website, these services receive your device’s IP address and basic technical information when you connect. We use them only to provide the game.

Sponsors

The game may show sponsor names or logos. Sponsors receive no information about you. Tapping a sponsor opens their website, which has its own privacy policy.

Where data is stored

Account and profile data are stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), which may process data outside the Maldives. Settings, your guest progress and your blocked list are stored on your device using browser storage; we don’t use advertising cookies.

How long we keep data

Profile data is kept while your account is active and deleted when you delete your account. Reports and support messages are kept for up to 12 months, or longer only if needed to deal with a serious safety issue.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the game: Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your leaderboard entry and your saved progress. Guest players can remove the data on their phone by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it, and can ask us through Help & support to delete their guest leaderboard entry.

Children

Dhivehi Games is a general-audience collection of card games with no real-money betting. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
